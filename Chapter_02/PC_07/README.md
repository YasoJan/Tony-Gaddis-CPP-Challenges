# PC_07 - Ocean Levels

## Problem
Assuming the ocean's level is currently rising at about 1.5 millimeters 
per year, write a program that displays the projected rise in ocean levels 
over the following time periods:

## Expected Output

| Years | Projected Rise (mm) |
|-------|-------------------|
| 5 years | 7.5 mm |
| 7 years | 10.5 mm |
| 10 years | 15.0 mm |

## Variables

| Variable | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| `RISE_PER_YEAR` | Rate of ocean rise in mm per year (constant) |
| `rise_5` | Projected rise in 5 years |
| `rise_7` | Projected rise in 7 years |
| `rise_10` | Projected rise in 10 years |

## Concepts Practiced
- Constants
- Floating point arithmetic
- Formatted output with `iomanip`
